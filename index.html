Title of Biostimulant:

Ascophyllum nodosum 20% (liquid)

Sr. No. Ingredients Content (%)
1 Seaweed (Ascophyllum nodosum) extract per cent. by weight, minimum 20%

6 Soybean Two foliar applications at 750 ml/ha
7 Wheat Two foliar applications at 1000 ml/ha
